CharterEdge – Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026
Developer: Sash Ventures LLC

Overview

CharterEdge ("the app") is a practice quiz app for CFA Level I, II, and III candidates. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

CharterEdge is an independent study aid developed by Sash Ventures LLC and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by CFA Institute. CFA® and Chartered Financial Analyst® are registered trademarks owned by CFA Institute.

Data We Do Not Collect

CharterEdge does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not require an account, login, or email address to use the app.
  • We do not collect your name or contact information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not share any data with third parties, except as described below for subscription processing.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All quiz activity — session results, scores, streaks, topic performance, and app settings — is stored exclusively on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to Sash Ventures LLC or anyone else.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

CharterEdge offers an optional Pro subscription and one-time level unlocks, processed through Apple's App Store using RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat may process device identifiers and purchase/receipt data solely to validate purchases and restore entitlements — Sash Ventures LLC does not receive or store your payment information. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Push Notifications

The app may send optional local notifications (such as a daily practice question reminder). These are generated on-device and do not involve any server communication. You can disable them at any time in iPhone Settings → Notifications → CharterEdge.

Children's Privacy

CharterEdge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
